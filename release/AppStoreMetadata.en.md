## Description

Balance helps you see not just a to-do list, but the bigger picture of your life.

Create goals and connect them to concrete tasks. Plan your day, deal with overdue items, and mark work as completed. Start focus sessions with a local completion notification and track progress across your life areas.

The app includes:

- goals with progress, pause, completion, and archive states;
- tasks organized by priority, due date, and goal;
- a Today screen for daily planning;
- a focus timer and session history;
- analytics for completed tasks, focus, goals, and balance;
- local data storage with no account or advertising.

Your goals and tasks remain on your device. The app does not use trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.
