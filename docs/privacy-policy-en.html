Balance App Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Data used by the app

Balance stores the goals, tasks, recurrence schedules, habits, weekly reviews, life areas, balance ratings, and focus-session history you enter locally on your device. The app does not create an account or send this data to the developer.

Widget

To power the widget, the app stores a short Today summary in a protected container shared by the app and its widget. The summary stays on your device and is not sent to a server.

Analytics and third-party sharing

The app does not use advertising systems, trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, or sell user data. All analytics shown in the app is calculated on the device.

Local notifications

With your permission, the app creates a local notification when a focus session ends. No data is sent to a server for this purpose. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a limited technical event log and MetricKit system reports about crashes and hangs on the device. They do not contain your task or goal names and are not sent to the developer automatically. They are shared only if you export and send a diagnostic report yourself.

Storage and deletion

Data remains on your device until you edit, archive, or delete it using the app. You can remove all local data by deleting the app from your device. Your device backup may contain app data according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

Policy changes

If data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the corresponding app version is released. The current effective date appears at the top of this page.
